Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nature Frontiers (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use our digital services and tools for managing Nature Frontiers social media accounts, including the Nature Frontiers Publishing application (the “App”).

Important: The App is intended for authorised people who manage Nature Frontiers social media accounts. It is not intended to collect information from the general public through this website.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted by an account administrator, the App may process:

  • Facebook Page and Instagram professional account identifiers, names, usernames and account connection details;
  • content and media that an authorised user selects or submits for publishing, together with captions, hashtags and other publishing metadata;
  • publishing status, post identifiers, timestamps and error information returned by the relevant platform;
  • basic technical, security and operational logs needed to maintain the service.

We do not intentionally request passwords for Facebook or Instagram. Authentication is handled through Meta's own authorisation process.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above only as needed to connect authorised accounts, prepare and publish content that has been approved by the account owner or an authorised reviewer, check publishing results, troubleshoot errors, protect the App and maintain operational records.

3. Legal basis and permissions

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information on the basis of the relevant account administrator's instructions and authorisation, our legitimate operational interests, or another basis permitted by law. You can manage or withdraw platform permissions through the relevant Meta account settings. Withdrawing permissions may prevent connected features from working.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to Meta Platforms services when an authorised user connects an account or uses publishing features. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. We may also use infrastructure or service providers needed to operate the App; they should receive only information needed for their service and are expected to protect it.

5. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including operational recordkeeping, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the operational need. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, request correction or deletion where applicable, or ask us to stop processing information where the law provides that right. You may also remove the App's access through Meta's settings. To make a request, email naturefrontiers@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request”. Do not email passwords, access tokens or other login credentials.

7. Data deletion

To request deletion of information associated with use of the App, email naturefrontiers@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”. Include the Facebook Page or Instagram username involved and enough information for us to identify the relevant connection. Do not include passwords or access tokens. We will review and respond to the request and delete information that we are not required or permitted to retain. You can also remove the App from your Meta account settings to revoke future access.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a business and account-management tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly use it to collect personal information from children.

9. International processing and third-party services

Meta and other service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and applicable law. For Meta's information practices, see Meta's Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
